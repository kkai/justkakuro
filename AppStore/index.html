Just Kakuro

The crossword of sums.

Kakuro is a crossword with sums instead of words. Each run of white cells adds up to its clue, and no digit repeats inside a run. Just Kakuro teaches you the techniques experienced solvers use, then gets out of the way.

Common questions

Does every puzzle have exactly one solution?
Yes. Every board is checked before you see it, and every board can be finished by reasoning alone. You are never asked to guess.
What does the one-time purchase include?
Six more lessons, every practice drill, the hints that name and explain a technique, large boards, and your stats. It is a single purchase, not a subscription.
I bought it and it is asking me to buy again.
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchases. That asks the App Store to confirm your purchase on this device. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Puzzles are generated on your device. The only time the app needs a connection is to check or restore a purchase.
Where is my progress kept?
On your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your progress with it.
